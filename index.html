About the Clipper app

Clipper is a private tool used only by the Leonida Live team to publish our own edited clips to our own accounts. It cuts stream moments into vertical videos with captions, and after we review each video it uploads it to the account we connected. It does not access anyone else's account and is not offered to the public.
