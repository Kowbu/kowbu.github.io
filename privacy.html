Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Clipper is a private publishing tool used only by the Leonida Live team. This policy explains what it does with data.

What we access

  • When we connect our own TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, the platform gives the tool an access token so it can upload our videos and read basic account information (such as the account name) and the view counts of our own posts.
  • We do not access, collect or store data from other users or viewers.

How we store it

  • Access tokens are stored only on our own computer and are never shared or sold.
  • View counts of our own posts are stored locally to learn which clips perform well.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the connected platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and permissions). Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored tokens.
